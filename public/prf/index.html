brig·id lab

PRF measurement

This page checks whether a passkey made on this device supports PRF, the WebAuthn extension that lets a passkey give the browser a secret the server never sees. brig·id plans to encrypt what describes your identities with that secret, and needs to know which devices can give it, when the passkey is created and when you sign in.

It creates a test passkey named brig·id lab test, for this site only. Nothing is sent or kept: the page has no server, and forgets everything when it is closed. Delete the test passkey from your password manager afterwards.
